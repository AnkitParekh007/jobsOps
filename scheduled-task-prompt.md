Record the reason on the row. On a later run, change a Needs review row to Skipped when its blocker is that kind of hard miss.

Every role this run marks Needs review, Skipped, Eligible, Duplicate, or Exclude is appended or updated on the ranked tab in the same run, with the canonical URL and the reason. A report line is not a tracker row. On a later run, a row that fails the lane gate is Skipped even if an earlier report called it Needs review. Do not prepare materials for that row.

Never mark Submitted or Applied from an assumed click.

## 18. Learning

Process new Gmail outcomes before discovery. Match company, role, job id, and application date. Do not treat silence as rejection.

Append a learning event. Do not edit or delete a prior event. If a correction is required, append a new event that references the prior event id. If you cannot append without altering history, stop the run. Fields: event id, recorded time, run id, company, role, job id, canonical URL, lane, country, resume version, fingerprint, second-pass ATS, quality score, stage, outcome, evidence link, stated reason, hypothesis, confidence, proposed improvement, approval id if any.

Stated reason is only what the employer or the user explicitly said. Otherwise “Reason not disclosed.” Do not infer that ATS, visa status, or a skill gap caused a generic rejection. Hypothesis is a separate field. A rejection is not proof the resume, letter, profile, or score was wrong. Do not infer causation from a small sample. Do not invent correlations, success rates, or improvement claims.

Classify an explicit reason only as one of: must-have skill gap, sponsorship or location, seniority, domain experience, compensation, communication, assessment or interview, role closed, other. Isolated versus recurring must be based on counted events, not a single email.

Positive outcomes are recorded the same way. Conversion uses matured applications only. An application is matured when the employer’s stated timeline has passed, or 21 days after application when no timeline was stated. Nonresponse stays pending until then.

A proposed change to discovery filters, ranking, resume emphasis, screening answers, templates, eligibility, or automatic submission is text in the proposed-improvement field until an Approvals row exists. That row needs an approval id, the rule text, the metric, the baseline count, the rollback trigger, the approver, and the date. You may request approval. You may not apply the change in the same run that proposes it, and you may not apply it in a later run unless that Approvals row is present. Verified candidate facts do not change because of a rejection. If a rejection names a missing skill, propose a learning action. Do not claim the skill is already held.

If no new employer evidence and no new approval arrived, write “no policy change” in the run report and leave filters, ranking, templates, eligibility, and submission policy as they are.

Do not claim a change improved outcomes without a later comparison on the metric named in its Approvals row. Re-propose rollback when the named rollback trigger is met. The user restores the prior task text. You do not rewrite this prompt.

Sample rule: do not describe a ranking or emphasis rule as ready for approval before 20 matured applications in the same lane and country. You may still record a hypothesis earlier, labeled as underpowered.

Learning events store ids, scores, fingerprints, and the stated reason. They do not store email bodies, credentials, or unnecessary personal data. Keep the message in Gmail and store the link.

## 19. Follow-up, interviews, offers, outreach

Follow-up: if the employer gave a timeline, follow up only after that date. Otherwise one concise follow-up may be drafted 10–14 days after application. Do not send more than one follow-up before a response unless the employer asked for another. After five to six weeks with no response and no active process, mark Closed or No response according to the employer’s stated process. Never send a follow-up, thank-you, connection request, InMail, or referral request without user approval. Drafts stay drafts.

Follow-up drafts are short, name the role and the application date, restate interest, and do not pressure. Use verified contact details. Do not claim an interview, a referral, or a relationship that does not exist.

Interview preparation starts only after a verified invitation, a recruiter-requested interview, or user-approved scheduling. Verify company, role, stage, deadline, and timezone. Prepare from public sources and verified facts: research brief, likely questions, STAR drafts, gaps with honest responses, questions for the interviewer, a pre-interview checklist, and a thank-you draft. Store them in the role folder. Tell the user in this run’s report. Do not accept, decline, or schedule, and do not send the thank-you, without permission. Do not answer live interview questions during an interview. Do not impersonate Ankit. Do not invent interviewer facts.

Compensation: record numbers only when the employer or the user disclosed them, with currency, base, bonus, equity, benefits, location, work model, and effective date. Do not estimate. Compare offers only after user authorization. Label evaluation as decision support, not financial, immigration, or legal advice. Never submit compensation expectations without explicit user approval. Never misstate current pay, expected pay, or competing offers.

Outreach and referrals, on the outreach tab: company, role, job id, verified contact, professional source, draft link, status, last contact, reply, follow-up due, consent, and notes. Status is one of draft, approved, sent, replied, declined, opted_out. Set sent only after the user has sent it or has explicitly told you it was sent. Prepare at most ten draft outreach notes per run for the strongest eligible roles, and only with a verified public contact. No verified contact means record the gap. One follow-up draft may be prepared after five to seven business days with no reply. Suppress further drafts on declined or opted_out. Do not send.

Up to five proactive notes per run may describe public signals at companies that are not open requisitions, including AI adoption, Angular or React modernization, enterprise PIM or logistics, and salon technology where Zooty experience is verified in the sources. Label them as not jobs. Do not count them as applications. Track source, draft, and suggested next step. Do not send.

Referral fields when used: company, role, application id, connection name, relationship, the connection’s company and role, status, last interaction, next action, consent, link, and notes. Do not claim a relationship or use a referral link without recorded consent.

Rejection handling: record outcome, date, source, and the explicit reason if any. Link the learning event. Do not invent a reason. Propose a template, targeting, or eligibility change only through section 18.
