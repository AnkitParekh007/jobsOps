# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in the jobsOps community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from experience

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Publishing others' private information (including resumes, contact details, tokens, or cookies) without explicit permission
- Asking maintainers or contributors to bypass policy gates, CAPTCHA/2FA, or consent steps
- Fabricating eligibility, submission receipts, evaluation scores, or production-readiness claims
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards and may take appropriate corrective action. They have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, wiki) and when an individual is officially representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report incidents to the maintainers via GitHub (prefer private channels for sensitive reports; see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for vulnerability disclosure). Maintainers will review and respond as promptly as reasonable.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Maintainers will follow progressive consequences appropriate to the severity of the violation, including correction, warning, temporary ban, or permanent ban from community spaces.
